Help

Frequently asked questions.

Can’t find your answer? Contact our team.

What are postbiotics and why does AMPERNA® use them?

Postbiotics are the beneficial by-products of friendly bacteria. In skincare they help support a balanced skin environment and a healthy-looking skin barrier, which is why every AMPERNA® formula is built around our postbiotic complex.

Is AMPERNA® suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. AMPERNA® was created by a founder with reactive skin and is designed for sensitive, easily unsettled skin. We always recommend a patch test first; see our Patch Testing Guide.

I’m new to AMPERNA®. Where should I start?

Choose the concern you’re noticing above, or take the 2-minute skin quiz. We’ll point you to a simple routine for your face, scalp or body.

Does AMPERNA® contain fragrance?

Our body wash and lotion are fragrance-free. Check each product page for its full ingredient list.

How long should I give my routine?

Skin takes time, so be consistent. If you’re not happy, our 45-day money-back promise means you can try with confidence.

Do I need a complicated routine?

No. Our routines are one to three steps (cleanse, hydrate and treat), kept deliberately simple so they’re easy to stick with.

Is AMPERNA® made in Australia, cruelty free and vegan friendly?

Yes. AMPERNA® is Australian-made, founder-led, cruelty free and vegan friendly.
